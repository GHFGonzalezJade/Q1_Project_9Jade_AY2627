The Ultimate Beginner's Guide to Volleyball

Welcome to volleyball! Whether you are playing on the beach or the indoor court, this guide will help you understand the foundational rules, positions, and skills needed to get started.

1. Basic Rules and Objectives

The main goal in volleyball is to send the ball over the net and ground it on the opponent's court, while preventing the same from happening on your side.

The Three-Hit Rule

A team is allowed up to three touches to return the ball. No single player can hit the ball twice in a row.

Scoring

Games are typically played using rally scoring, meaning a point is scored on every single serve, regardless of which team served the ball.

2. Understanding the Court

Knowing the boundaries and key lines will help you navigate your movement during a rally.

The Attack Line (3-Meter Line)

This line separates the front row from the back row. Back-row players cannot jump and attack the ball above the net if they are inside this line.

3. Volleyball Positions

There are six players on the court at a time, each fulfilling a specific tactical role.

The Setter

Considered the "quarterback" of the team. The setter's primary job is to run the offense and set up the hitters for an attack.

The Libero

A defensive specialist who wears a different colored jersey. They play strictly in the back row and excel at passing and digging.

Essential Volleyball Equipment

Regulation Volleyball

Must be spherical, made of flexible leather, with a circumference of 65-67 cm.

Volleyball Net

Standard net heights are 2.43m (7'11 5/8") for men and 2.24m (7'4 1/8") for women.

Knee Pads

Crucial protective gear for defensive digs and dives on hard courts.
